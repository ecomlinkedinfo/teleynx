The Real Problem

It was never your product. It was your budget.

To find a winning product, the algorithm needs data, and data costs ad spend.

Most beginners start with a few hundred dollars, burn through it before the algorithm even learns, and quit, convinced dropshipping is dead. It isn't. They just ran out of money before it could work.

The pros aren't smarter than you. They just kept on testing and learning until they understood it.

That's the exact problem we remove. We fund the testing, so you reach a winner without gambling your own savings.

FAQs

Is this a scam? How do you actually fund this?

EcomLinked generates revenue through a program fee and a performance-based profit share on stores we help scale. Our capital pool comes from company reserves that are topped off with membership fees. Our incentives are fully aligned with your profit. We're a registered business with verifiable partners (Shopify, Meta).

Do I need prior e-commerce experience?

No. The majority of our funded partners started with zero e-commerce knowledge. Our 8-week curriculum is designed for complete beginners. What matters more than experience is your willingness to follow the system, put in the hours, and stay coachable.

How much time do I need to commit weekly?

During the 8-week build phase, expect to invest 10-15 hours per week. This includes product research, store setup, creative creation, and ad management learning. Once your store is live and funded, you can manage it in 5-10 hours per week with our mentorship support.

What happens if my ads don't work? Do I owe the ad spend back?

No. The ad budget isn't a loan, so there's nothing to repay. If a campaign doesn't work, we pause it, learn from it, and pivot. And if a store just doesn't pan out after real effort, we move on to the next,

How quickly can I get funded after joining?

Fast. Once you're accepted into a cohort, your funding usually lands within 2 to 3 days. No waiting months to prove yourself first. We back you upfront so you can start testing right away.
